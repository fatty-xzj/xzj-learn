Academic Profile of Zhijie Xu
Hello, I'm Zhijie Xu (徐智杰) 
I'm a master's student in Agricultural Engineering and Information Technology at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (NUIST), China.

My research focuses on agricultural remote sensing, particularly winter wheat mapping, crop phenology, and satellite time-series analysis.

I'm interested in combining multi-source satellite observations, deep learning, and crop growth modeling to improve crop identification and monitoring.
Research Interests
- Agricultural remote sensing: winter wheat identification and crop monitoring
- Satellite time series: Sentinel-2 and multi-source data fusion
- Phenology-aware learning: temporal representation and crop growth patterns
- Deep learning: semantic segmentation and small-field crop mapping
- Crop modeling: exploring the integration of WOFOST and remote sensing
Research Projects
- Phenology-aware Winter Wheat Mapping — ongoing research
- Cropping Intensity Mapping from NDVI Time Series — ongoing research
- Crop Growth Modeling with WOFOST — ongoing research
Tools & Skills
